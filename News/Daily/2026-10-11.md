# Daily News — 2026-10-11

Coverage window: 2026-10-10 06:27 to 2026-10-11 06:27 (Europe/Zurich)

## Overview

A growing US–Ukrainian dispute and further attacks on Saudi aviation complicated diplomacy, while enforcement of the Iran blockade continued at sea. Palestinian elections were formally postponed, tensions between Ethiopia and Eritrea moved to the UN, and European budget and electoral debates sharpened. An Arctic shipping milestone, possible consolidation in AI and renewed opposition to capital punishment highlighted competing economic and political priorities.

## 1. Trump calls for replacing Zelenskyy after a deadly Russian strike

President Donald Trump called for Ukraine to replace President Volodymyr Zelenskyy and stop attacks on Russian energy infrastructure. Ukrainian officials said a Russian strike on Zaporizhzhia killed 20 people, including three children; Zelenskyy criticised Washington’s removal of diesel sanctions, while European allies reaffirmed support for Kyiv.

**Why it matters:** The dispute threatens coordination over peace negotiations and the economic pressure applied to Moscow.

## 2. New Riyadh airport attack kills 12 as Washington considers intervention

Saudi authorities said a further attack on Riyadh’s international airport killed at least 12 people and wounded more than 300. Trump said the United States might join Saudi strikes against the Houthis; Reuters reported that the group had not claimed this latest attack.

**Why it matters:** The new strike raises the risk of wider military involvement and further disruption to civilian aviation.

**Topics:** [Houthis](../../Topics/Houthis.md), [Yemen Conflict](../../Topics/Yemen-Conflict.md), [Iran](../../Topics/Iran.md)

## 3. US military disables cargo vessel attempting to pass Iran blockade

US Central Command said a fighter jet disabled the propulsion of a Panama-flagged cargo vessel in the Gulf of Oman after its crew ignored blockade warnings. CENTCOM said no crew members were harmed; Reuters could not immediately obtain comment from the operator.

**Why it matters:** The incident shows that military enforcement remains a direct hazard for commercial shipping despite diplomatic discussions.

**Topics:** [Iran](../../Topics/Iran.md), [Strait of Hormuz](../../Topics/Strait-of-Hormuz.md), [United States](../../Topics/United-States.md)

**Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-military-says-it-disabled-cargo-vessel-trying-run-iran-blockade-2026-10-11/)

## 4. Abbas formally postpones elections and schedules a presidential poll

Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas issued a decree postponing November’s legislative elections and scheduling legislative and presidential votes for September 11, 2027. The decree cited Israeli restrictions and conditions in Gaza; Israel’s foreign minister rejected that explanation.

**Why it matters:** The formal decision replaces yesterday’s reported intention with a new timetable for renewing Palestinian political mandates.

**Topics:** [Palestinian Politics](../../Topics/Palestinian-Politics.md), [Israel](../../Topics/Israel.md)

**Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/abbas-postpones-palestinian-elections-sets-presidential-poll-september-2027-2026-10-10/)

## 5. Eritrea seeks UN action as Ethiopian forces enter Adigrat

Eritrea asked the UN Security Council for deterrent measures against Ethiopia, rejecting Ethiopian allegations of an Eritrean incursion. Residents reported Ethiopian forces and allied militia in Adigrat on Saturday; Reuters verified footage of allied militia in the city but could not independently verify the alleged incursion.

**Why it matters:** The diplomatic confrontation and troop movements heighten the risk of interstate war in the Horn of Africa.

**Topics:** [Ethiopia](../../Topics/Ethiopia.md), [United Nations](../../Topics/United-Nations.md)

**Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/africa/ethiopian-forces-regain-control-border-city-after-reported-eritrean-incursion-2026-10-10/)

## 6. Irish EU presidency proposes an 8% cut to the next budget plan

Ireland’s EU presidency proposed reducing the Commission’s 2028–2034 budget plan by 8%, to roughly €1.6 trillion in 2025 prices. Germany, Sweden and the Netherlands sought greater restraint, while European Parliament representatives opposed the proposed reduction.

**Why it matters:** The compromise exposes competing demands for defence, competitiveness, agricultural and regional funding before EU leaders meet on October 15–16.

**Topics:** [European Union](../../Topics/European-Union.md)

**Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/eus-irish-presidency-wants-8-cut-proposed-2028-2034-budget-plan-2026-10-10/)

## 7. New centrist party enters Spain’s snap-election contest

Lawyer Miriam Gonzalez Durantez launched Democracy21, aiming to field candidates in Madrid and Barcelona in Spain’s scheduled November 29 election. Participation depends on obtaining sufficient endorsements, and the launch follows the government’s defeat over housing measures.

**Why it matters:** The new entrant tests demand for an alternative to established political blocs amid the housing crisis.

**Topics:** [Spain](../../Topics/Spain.md)

**Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/spanish-lawyer-gonzalez-durantez-launches-centrist-party-november-election-2026-10-10/)

## 8. South Korea completes its first commercial Arctic round trip to Europe

The PanStar Acro returned to Busan after completing South Korea’s first commercial round trip to Europe through Arctic waters. Officials hope for regular services by 2030, but the Russian coastal route poses diplomatic difficulties as well as ice, insurance and scheduling risks.

**Why it matters:** The completed trial advances a government-backed shipping strategy with consequences for relations with Russia and Western allies.

**Topics:** [South Korea](../../Topics/South-Korea.md), [Russia](../../Topics/Russia.md)

**Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/south-korea-completes-arctic-europe-commercial-voyage-eyes-regular-service-2026-10-11/)

## 9. Nvidia discusses deeper investment in or acquisition of Reflection AI

The Financial Times reported that Nvidia was discussing a larger investment in Reflection AI or a possible acquisition, according to Reuters. Talks remain preliminary and could fail; possible structures include hiring staff and licensing technology rather than buying the company outright.

**Why it matters:** The discussions could deepen ties between a major AI-chip supplier and an open-model developer.

**Topics:** [Artificial Intelligence](../../Topics/Artificial-Intelligence.md), [Semiconductors](../../Topics/Semiconductors.md)

**Sources:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/nvidia-talks-invest-further-reflection-ai-or-buy-it-ft-reports-2026-10-10/)

## 10. Pope Leo renews opposition to capital punishment amid execution-broadcast dispute

Pope Leo condemned the death penalty on Saturday, saying justice can be achieved without executions. His message did not name the Pentagon’s plan to broadcast Nidal Hasan’s scheduled December execution; Trump has said he has not decided whether to permit the feed.

**Why it matters:** The intervention adds religious opposition to a contested proposal with international human-rights implications.
