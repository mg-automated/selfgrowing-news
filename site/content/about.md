# About this archive

Self-growing News is a non-commercial, AI-assisted archive of developments in international politics, Swiss politics, and technology. An automated workflow reviews recent reporting, selects ten substantive developments for each daily briefing, and produces concise summaries with links to the reporting and primary sources used.

The archive is intended to provide orientation and preserve a connected record over time. It is not a substitute for the original reporting, and inclusion does not imply endorsement of a source, person, organisation, or political position.

AI-assisted research and summarisation can produce errors, omissions, or outdated information despite source checks. Readers should verify important claims using the linked original sources. Nothing in this archive constitutes legal, financial, investment, or other professional advice.

## Corrections

Factual errors, misleading summaries, and broken source links should be reported through the repository's [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/mg-automated/selfgrowing-news/issues). Corrections are made in the relevant Markdown file, and the repository's Git history preserves the resulting change.

When reporting an issue, please identify the daily briefing and story, describe the concern, and include a supporting source where possible. Do not include private or sensitive personal information.
